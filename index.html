Stick to the Rules Publisher

Stick to the Rules is an animated story channel on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube (sticktotherules.pages.dev). The Publisher is the channel's own tool: it uploads the episodes we make to our own TikTok account at the time we set, with the AI-generated content label on.

Who uses it

Only the Stick to the Rules team, for the channel's own account. It is not offered to other TikTok users and has no sign-up.

What it does on TikTok

  • Logs in once with TikTok Login Kit, with the account owner's consent.
  • Reads the account's basic profile and posting options (privacy levels, maximum video length).
  • Uploads a finished episode, either to the account's inbox to be finished in the TikTok app, or as a post with the caption, cover frame and AI-generated label we set.
