Privacy Policy

Stick to the Rules Publisher · last updated 27 October 2026

What we receive from TikTok

When the account owner logs in with TikTok, the Publisher receives the account's open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), an access token and a refresh token, and the account's posting options (privacy levels, comment setting, maximum video length).

What we use it for

Only to upload the channel's own videos to that same account (scopes video.upload and video.publish). We do not read other users' data, comments, followers or messages.

Where it is kept

The tokens are stored on the channel's own server, readable only by the Publisher, and are never shared, sold or shown to anyone. Videos are kept on that server until they are posted.

How to remove it

Remove the Publisher's access in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions, or email us and we delete the stored tokens within 7 days.
