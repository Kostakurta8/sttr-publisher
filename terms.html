1. What this is

Stick to the Rules Publisher ("the Publisher") is a private tool of the Stick to the Rules channel. It posts videos made by the channel to the channel's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

2. Who may use it

Only the Stick to the Rules team, on accounts the channel owns. There is no public sign-up and no other user accounts.

3. Content

Every video the Publisher posts is made and owned by Stick to the Rules. The channel is responsible for it and follows TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Videos made with AI tools are posted with TikTok's AI-generated content label.

4. TikTok

The Publisher uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API under TikTok's Developer Terms. TikTok can change or end that access at any time. The account owner can remove the Publisher's access in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions.

The Publisher is provided as is, for the channel's own use.
